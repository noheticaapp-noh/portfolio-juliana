Fonapp

Brand, Id, UI/UX Design

Partner: IMIP Hospital, speech therapist's clinic and Unicap, Internet Systems course. (Pro-Bono project).

Project

Application to assist in speech therapist treatment at a distance, based on the limitations imposed by the new situation resulting from the pandemic, a speech therapist clinic at IMIP was unable to continue the treatment, especially for patients who had undergone cleft lip and palate surgery. Our proposal was to create a remote treatment environment that encompassed other professionals and all possible conditions to be treated by speech therapists.
